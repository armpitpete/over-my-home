What “within hearing distance” means

The app combines the aircraft’s horizontal distance and altitude to estimate its straight-line distance from the postcode. Weather, aircraft type, buildings and local noise can make a real aircraft easier or harder to hear.

Live position data from OpenSky Network. UK postcode coordinates from Postcodes.io. Your postcode is used for the request and saved only in this browser.
